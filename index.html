About

I’m Clarence Crowell, a photographer and filmmaker in Chicago. At FRET12 I make the campaign films, the ads, the live music sessions and a documentary series about local makers, most of them from the first idea through camera, edit and color. I also shoot nearly all of the brand’s photography.

I co-founded Good Fall Studio, a climbing apparel brand, and I write fiction. Pick a section below, or get in touch at the bottom of the page.
