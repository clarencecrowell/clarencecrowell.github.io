About

Landscape, wildlife, climbing, live music, and the product and campaign work I shoot for FRET12.

Most of the landscape work comes from trips west: Grand Teton and Yellowstone, Rocky Mountain National Park, and the rock around Colorado’s Front Range. Dispersed is a photo book I made from the Yellowstone and Teton trip, shot on film.

Nearly every photo on fret12.com is mine. Each launch gets a photo set that goes with its film: I plan it with FRET12’s creative director, then shoot, edit and publish it.
