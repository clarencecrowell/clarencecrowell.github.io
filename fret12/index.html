About

FRET12 is a Chicago brand built around guitars and touring culture, and a lot of what it sells is designed and sewn in its own studio. I’ve been its content creator since 2023.

The films on this page are mine from start to finish: I came up with the idea, then shot, edited and color graded each one. The photography that goes with every launch is mine too.

Outside the camera work I built ThankVids, an AI-assisted tool that cut one of FRET12’s video-fulfillment jobs from about 20 minutes to 45 seconds a unit. It’s now being developed as its own product.
