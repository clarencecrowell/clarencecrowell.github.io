About

Back to Craft is a documentary series from FRET12 about local makers and the work they do by hand.

FRET12’s creative directors came up with the series. I direct, produce, shoot, edit, color and publish the episodes.

Each episode stays close to the work: the shop, the tools, and the person explaining what they make and why.
