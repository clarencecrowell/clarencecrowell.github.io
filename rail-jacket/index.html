About

The Rail Jacket was designed, patterned, cut and sewn by FRET12 for Huckberry, then naturally dyed with coffee from Metric Coffee.

I came up with the concept for the campaign, then shot, edited and color graded the hero film and the recap of the launch event at Huckberry’s Chicago store.

I also shot, edited and published the campaign photography, including the Instagram stories below.
