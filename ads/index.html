About

I run FRET12’s Meta ads and make the creative that goes in them.

Most of it is vertical and built for the feed: a hook in the first second, a retargeting unboxing with Mark Tremonti, product launches with 2D motion graphics. I concept, shoot, edit and color every piece, then set up and manage the campaigns.

The ads below play on their own as you scroll, muted, the way they would in a feed. Tap one for sound.
