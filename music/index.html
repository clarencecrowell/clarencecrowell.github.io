About

FRET12 Sessions are live sets filmed with touring and local bands for FRET12’s YouTube channel. I’ve shot every episode.

The format comes from FRET12’s creative directors. My job is camera, edit and color: covering a full band playing live, then cutting and grading the set.

I also film lessons for The Sound and The Story, FRET12’s long-running artist series.
