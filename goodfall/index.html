About

Good Fall Studio is a climbing-culture apparel brand I co-founded and run end to end: product, storefront, content and strategy.

I rebuilt the Shopify store and the SEO across the whole catalog, and designed Good Fall Vol. 1, a 92-page print magazine, in InDesign.

The rest is content: short climbing films, vertical ads for drops, and photos from comps, gym sessions and trips to the Red River Gorge, Jackson Falls and Southern Illinois.
